01 / PRODUCT POSITION

Systems hold records.
Rivet holds the handoffs.

Hiring, onboarding, transfers and departures cross teams and systems. The point of failure is often not missing data but an unclear next action, an unapproved step or an employee identity that was silently mismatched.

Rivet connects staged source records to a durable execution layer. People remain accountable for identity matches, workflow publication and high-impact approvals; every result has a recorded state.

The platform deliberately leaves payroll, core HR records and device provisioning with systems built for those responsibilities.

04 / GOVERNANCE

AI drafts.
People decide.

Rivet makes a structural distinction between preparing work and authorizing it. Structured AI suggestions can be reviewed and edited, but cannot publish a workflow, approve an offboarding step or make a customer-data update by themselves.

Architecture and safeguards

Staged identity matchingSource records are linked intentionally, not merged silently.
